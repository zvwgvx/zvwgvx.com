Research and engineering start with questions: how should a long-lived artificial entity represent its identity, what makes an autonomous security system trustworthy, or how can independent machines coordinate reliably?

This space is for occasional writing about those questions and the principles behind the systems I study.

## What belongs here

- **Research questions:** problems, hypotheses and perspectives on cognitive AI and autonomy.
- **Architecture principles:** design choices and trade-offs with broader relevance.
- **Public findings:** research conclusions or work I deliberately choose to publish.
- **Essays:** reflections on security research, networking and systems engineering.

This is not a weekly progress report or a record of internal implementation. My [Work](/work/) page introduces each research direction without requiring continual status updates.
