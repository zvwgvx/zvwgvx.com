Research and software engineering involve different kinds of evidence. A design can be interesting without being implemented. A system can pass local tests without being stable. An experimental result can be promising without generalizing.

This notebook is intended to make those distinctions explicit. I plan to use it for work on cognitive architectures, autonomous agents, security tooling, and distributed infrastructure.

## What belongs here

- **Research questions:** what a system is trying to understand, and which assumptions remain open.
- **Architecture notes:** trade-offs, alternatives, and why particular boundaries were selected.
- **Implementation details:** what has actually been built and tested.
- **Limitations:** failures, missing evaluations, and work that has not yet been verified.

The goal is useful technical documentation, not a stream of announcements. Major work and source links are collected on the [Work](/work/) page.
